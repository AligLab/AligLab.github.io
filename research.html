Research direction 01

Lymphoma biology & heterogeneity

Which molecular features distinguish lymphoma subtypes?

Molecular profiling

We combine genomic profiling and computational analysis to investigate molecular heterogeneity and the biological differences between lymphomas.

Our work connects molecular profiles with questions about lymphoma classification and behavior. Noninvasive profiling offers a way to investigate this diversity and follow molecular changes over time.

Research direction 02

Liquid biopsy & molecular monitoring

How can tumor-derived DNA help us monitor lymphoma more precisely?

Circulating tumor DNA

We develop sensitive circulating tumor DNA approaches to study lymphoma through blood samples and assess molecular changes during treatment.

Circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) is tumor-derived DNA found in blood. Our research investigates how this information can support disease characterization, response assessment, and the detection of minimal residual disease.

Related publications

  • Remission Assessment by Circulating Tumor DNA in Large B-Cell LymphomaJournal of Clinical Oncology · 2025
  • Enhanced detection of minimal residual disease by targeted sequencing of phased variants in circulating tumor DNANature Biotechnology · 2021

Research direction 03

Treatment resistance & clonal evolution

How does lymphoma evolve during therapy, and what contributes to resistance?

Clonal evolution and resistance

We study clonal evolution and resistance mechanisms using genomic analysis and pre-clinical functional models.

By investigating how lymphoma changes over time, we aim to understand the biological mechanisms associated with treatment resistance. Experimental models complement molecular observations and help us explore clinically relevant questions.

Related publications

  • Determinants of resistance to engineered T cell therapies targeting CD19 in large B cell lymphomasCancer Cell · 2023

Research terminology

What is a liquid biopsy?

A liquid biopsy examines material in a body fluid, such as blood, to obtain information about disease. Our research focuses on tumor-derived DNA.

What is circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA)?

Circulating tumor DNA is DNA released by tumor cells that can be found in blood. We study its molecular features and changes over time.

What is minimal residual disease?

Minimal residual disease refers to small amounts of disease remaining after treatment. We investigate molecular approaches to its detection in lymphoma.
