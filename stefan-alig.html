About Stefan Alig

Stefan Alig is a hematologist and medical oncologist at University Hospital Essen, where he has served as Senior Attending Physician and Max-Eder Research Group Leader since 2025.

He trained at LMU Munich and spent five years as a postdoctoral research fellow at Stanford University with Ash A. Alizadeh and Maximilian Diehn. His work focuses on liquid biopsy, molecular classification, and precision oncology in lymphoma.
